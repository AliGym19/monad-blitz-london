The fuse, with the lid off.

This is the Standing-Restricted Intelligence: the layer that reads contradictions, compresses them, and decides — under a written policy it cannot amend. Every step below is recomputed live from the chain. Ask it why, and it answers from the ledger, not from a model. What it cannot prove, it refuses to say.
